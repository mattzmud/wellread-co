Everything a book lover needs

All in one beautifully simple place.

📚

Your Personal Library

Track every book you own, have read, are currently reading, or want to read next — all in one organized collection.

📖

Reading Log

Log your reading sessions with start and finish dates, format (read or listened), and mark books as completed or DNF.

⭐

Reviews & Ratings

Write personal reviews, give star ratings, and optionally publish them to share your thoughts with the community.

🎯

Monthly Reading Goals

Set a reading goal each month and track your progress. Look back at your goal history and celebrate every milestone.

🏅

Badges & Achievements

Earn badges for reaching milestones — reading 10, 25, or 100 books, writing reviews, meeting goals, and more.

🔖

To Be Read List

Keep a prioritized list of books you plan to read next. Drag and drop to arrange them in the order you want to tackle them.

⭐

Public Wishlist

Build a shareable wishlist of books you'd love to own. Send the link to friends and family — perfect for gift ideas.

👥

Book Clubs

Create or join book clubs with wall posts, events, RSVPs, and discussions. Invite members and coordinate your next great read together.

🤝

Connect with Readers

Find and follow other readers, see what your friends are reading, and share your reading life with people who love books as much as you do.

✉️

Direct Messaging

Chat with fellow readers, share book recommendations, and talk about what you're reading — all within the app.

📖

Lending Tracker

Track which books you've lent out and to whom. Never lose track of a loaned book again.

🔔

Friend Activity Feed

See what your friends are reading, finishing, and reviewing in real time — discover your next book through the people you trust.
